STANDARD REFERRAL 01

Every confirmed referral counts.

Earn a reward for each successful customer referral, regardless of the portfolio package they purchase.

PER SUCCESSFUL REFERRAL R100
  • Earn an additional R400 on every third successful referral.
  • The third-referral cycle repeats at referrals 3, 6, 9, 12 and so on.
  • Three qualifying referrals earn R700 total: R300 in referral rewards plus the R400 milestone bonus.

Milestone example 3 referrals = R700

PROFESSIONAL & EXECUTIVE SALES 02

A milestone for a specific mix.

Earn R100 for each successful Professional or Executive customer referral.

FOUR REFERRALS IN THE QUALIFYING MIX R1,400
  • Qualify after referring two Professional customers and two Executive customers.
  • The four R100 referral rewards total R400; the qualifying mix earns a separate R1,000 milestone bonus.
  • The R1,000 bonus is separate from the Standard programme’s R400 third-referral bonus.

Qualifying mix 2 Professional + 2 Executive = R1,400

04 · PROGRAMME TERMS

Built on clear
qualification.

Only genuine referrals that result in confirmed, qualifying customer purchases count toward referral rewards and programme milestones.

Refunded or cancelled orders do not qualify. Referral tracking instructions and payout arrangements will be explained to approved partners before they begin referring customers.

Programme rewards are subject to verification of the referral, selected package and customer payment. Applying does not guarantee approval or partner status.
