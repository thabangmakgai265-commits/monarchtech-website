01 / About

Engineering Intelligence.
Advancing Human Potential.

I am a Professor of Mechatronics Engineering working at the intersection of robotics, intelligent systems, automation and human-centred engineering.

My research explores how intelligent machines can operate safely, efficiently and effectively within complex environments. My work brings together engineering, artificial intelligence, automation and systems thinking to investigate challenges in modern industry.

I contribute to postgraduate education through research supervision, research-led teaching and interdisciplinary academic collaboration. I work with students and research partners to develop practical approaches to emerging engineering challenges.

My academic work focuses on the development and application of intelligent engineering systems, with particular interest in robotics, automation, human–robot interaction and intelligent technologies.
